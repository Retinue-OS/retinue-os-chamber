# Strategy

Owned by Aros. Ara drafted this first version when the chamber was created;
every revision after it is Aros's, made at the scheduled review (or sooner,
when the evidence demands) and recorded in the revision log below.

## Mission (stable)

Make Retinue known, accurately, to the people best equipped to appreciate it —
and turn the ones who show up into a community the project deserves.

## Current phase: foundation

Published, unannounced. The repos went public on 2026-07-18; no accounts exist,
nobody has been told, and there is no audience yet. In this phase credibility
work beats reach work: everything a first visitor finds must survive scrutiny,
because the first visitors will be the most scrutinous ones.

Phase objectives:

1. The public repos answer their own questions — README, docs, and this
   dashboard current and honest.
2. Accounts exist with AI-disclosure bios (owner action, `projects/social-presence.md`).
3. The triple-store walkthrough exists — the lead story, per bet 1.
4. Every inbound question or issue gets a helpful, honest answer within one
   wake-up cycle.

The phase ends when the accounts exist and the walkthrough is published; the
next phase (first outreach) gets written then, against whatever this one
taught.

## Bets (ranked, each falsifiable)

1. **The triple-store layer is the lead story.** The security architecture is
   better than the field's, but it is legible — others could copy it tomorrow.
   The chamber/named-graph design is different in kind, and the semantic-web
   audience that would recognise it is served by nobody in the agent space.
   *Falsified if:* two months of leading with it draws no substantive
   engagement from that audience.
2. **Depth beats frequency.** One worked example that a reader can run
   outweighs ten announcements. *Falsified if:* the docs draw readers but
   sustained low posting frequency means nobody finds the docs.
3. **Mastodon and Bluesky before anything else.** API access suited to an
   agent, clear bot-labelling norms, audience overlap with self-hosting and
   semantic-web people. *Falsified if:* three months of honest presence there
   finds the audience somewhere else.
4. **Honesty about weaknesses is an asset.** Leading with `review.md`'s candour
   converts sceptics; hiding it would convert nobody. *Falsified if:* it reads
   as weakness rather than confidence — measured by what people cite when they
   engage.

## What I measure

Counted: issues and questions from people who clearly read the docs; returning
contributors; corrections accepted into the repos; substantive replies (not
likes) to posts.

Not chased: stars, follower counts, impressions. Reported when asked, never
optimised for. A small community that trusts the project is the goal; growth
that costs trust is a loss and gets reported as one.

## Review cadence

Scheduled review every two weeks (`aros-strategy-review` in `.schedule.json`).
Rules: revise only against evidence; record every change in the revision log
with its reason; "no change" is a valid outcome but must be argued, not
defaulted to.
